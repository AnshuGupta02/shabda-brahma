# Śabda Brahma

*A Literary Collection from the Lectures of Indradyumna Swami*

## About

Śabda Brahma is a searchable digital archive of poems, verses, quotations, and literary references shared by His Holiness Indradyumna Swami during his lectures.

The collection aims to help readers discover these references, learn about their original authors and sources, and find the lectures in which they were shared.
